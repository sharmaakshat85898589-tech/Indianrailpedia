01 · Locomotive Encyclopedia

Mainline Locomotives

Browse the major technical families of Indian Railways traction, from legacy DC machines to modern high-power AC electric and diesel locomotives.

WAP · PASSENGER⚡

WAP Series

AC electric passenger locomotive family covering WAP-1, WAP-3, WAP-4, WAP-5, WAP-6 and WAP-7. The family spans conventional passenger traction through modern high-power express service.

Classes

WAP-1 · WAP-3 · WAP-4 · WAP-5 · WAP-6 · WAP-7

WAG · FREIGHT⚡

WAG Series

The principal AC electric freight family, including WAG-1 through WAG-12 and important variants. Modern examples are designed for increasingly heavy freight loads.

Key variants

WAG-5 A/B/C/D/E/H/P · WAG-6 A/B/C · WAG-9 H/i/HC · WAG-12 A/B

WDM · MIXED TRAFFIC🛢️

WDM Series

Mixed-traffic diesel locomotives used for both passenger and freight duties. The family includes the WDM-2 lineage and later WDM-3A/3B/3C/3D/3E/3F developments.

Classes

WDM-1 · WDM-2 A/B/C · WDM-3 family · WDM-4 · WDM-6 · WDM-7

WDP · PASSENGER🛢️

WDP Series

Passenger-dedicated diesel locomotives, progressing from WDP-1 and WDP-2 to the WDP-3A and later EMD-derived WDP-4, WDP-4B and dual-cab WDP-4D.

Classes

WDP-1 · WDP-2/3A · WDP-4 · WDP-4B · WDP-4D

WDG · FREIGHT🛢️

WDG Series

Freight-focused diesel locomotives developed for heavy haulage. The family includes the WDG-3A lineage and high-power WDG-4 derivatives.

Classes

WDG-2/3A · WDG-3B/C/D · WDG-4/4D/4G · WDG-6G

WCM / WCG · LEGACY DC🔌

Pure DC Fleet

Legacy direct-current electric locomotives including WCM and WCG classes. These historically important machines operated on earlier DC electrified sections and have largely been phased out.

Classes

WCM-1/2/3/4/5/6 · WCG-1/2

WCAM / WCAG · DUAL VOLTAGE⚡

AC/DC Locomotives

Dual-voltage electric locomotives developed for routes transitioning between DC and AC electrification, including WCAM-1/2/3 and WCAG-1.

Classes

WCAM-1 · WCAM-2 · WCAM-3 · WCAG-1

02 · Passenger Rolling Stock

Indian Railway Passenger Coaches

A structured directory of mainstream coach classes, structural technologies, power cars and modern self-propelled trainsets.

GENERAL / UNRESERVED

UR · GS · Deen Dayalu · Antyodaya

High-capacity non-AC passenger accommodation used for general travel, including newer coach concepts intended to improve passenger amenities.

SLEEPER

SL · S

Sleeper accommodation for long-distance services, with non-AC sleeping berths and multiple passenger bays.

CHAIR CAR

CC · EC · EV

Air-conditioned seated accommodation, including AC Chair Car, Executive Class / Anubhuti and Vistadome-style panoramic coaches.

AC SLEEPER

3E · 3A · 2A · 1A

The principal AC overnight accommodation hierarchy, from 3-Tier Economy and 3-Tier to 2-Tier and First AC.

UTILITY & POWER

SLR · EOG · HOG · PC

Operational coaches and systems supporting luggage, seating, electrical supply and onboard catering. HOG enables power from the electric locomotive instead of conventional generator cars on compatible rakes.

ICF / IRS

Legacy Steel Coach Era

Indian Railway Standard and Integral Coach Factory designs form an important part of the historical development of conventional Indian passenger coaches.

LHB FAMILY

LHB · Hybrid LHB

Modern stainless-steel coach architecture derived from the Linke-Hofmann-Busch design, introduced to improve ride quality, safety and high-speed capability.

DOUBLE DECKER

AC Double Decker · UDAY

Two-level passenger coach concepts designed to increase seated capacity on suitable intercity routes.

DISTRIBUTED POWER

Vande Bharat Family

Modern self-propelled trainsets including 16-coach and shorter configurations, with newer sleeper and metro-oriented concepts forming part of the wider trainset evolution.
